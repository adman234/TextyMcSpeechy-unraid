Training

    

      

        
        
        
      

      
Bigger batches train faster until VRAM runs out. Leave
        "from scratch" off — fine-tuning a pretrained checkpoint is far quicker.

      

      
Training picks up from its own latest checkpoint, so
        stopping it — or restarting the container — costs you at most the
        epochs since the last save. Tick "start over" only if you have changed
        the clips and want to discard the epochs trained on the old ones.
